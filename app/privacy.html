隐私说明

适用于 paper2zh 网站与 Chrome 扩展 · 更新于 2026 年 11 月 17 日

paper2zh 没有服务器，也没有账号系统。论文的解析、排版和缓存都在你的浏览器里完成，开发者收不到你的任何数据。

论文内容去了哪里

为了翻译和生成总结，paper2zh 会把论文的文字从你的浏览器直接发给你在“模型设置”里选的服务：默认是 Google 翻译，也可以是 Anthropic（Claude）、OpenAI、DeepSeek、Google Gemini、OpenRouter，或你自己填写的兼容接口。paper2zh 发送这些文字只为完成翻译和总结；对方服务如何处理它们，以该服务的隐私政策为准。图片、图表和 PDF 文件本身不会发出去。

API Key

你填写的 API Key 保存在本机浏览器的 localStorage 里，只在请求对应服务时作为身份凭证发给该服务，不会发给其他任何地方。

本地缓存

译文和总结缓存在本机浏览器的 IndexedDB 里，下次打开同一篇论文时直接读取。清除该网站的数据，或卸载扩展，缓存随之删除。

扩展读取哪些网页

  • 只有你在某个标签页上点击 paper2zh 图标（或在链接上用右键菜单打开）时，扩展才获得这个标签页的临时访问权（activeTab），用来以你在该网站的登录状态下载这篇 PDF。
  • 如果直接下载失败，扩展会请你确认后再访问那个网站；不确认就不访问。授权可以随时在 Chrome 的扩展管理页撤销。
  • 扩展不读取浏览记录，不在你浏览的网页里注入广告或统计代码，也不在后台访问任何网页。

统计与广告

paper2zh 不含统计、追踪或广告代码，不出售、不转让任何用户数据。

Privacy Policy

Applies to the paper2zh website and Chrome extension · Updated November 17, 2026

paper2zh has no server and no accounts. Parsing, layout and caching all happen in your browser, and the developer receives none of your data.

Paper text

To translate and summarize, paper2zh sends the text of the paper from your browser directly to the service you choose in Settings: Google Translate by default, or Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or a compatible endpoint you enter. paper2zh sends it only to produce the translation and summary; how the chosen service handles it is governed by that service's privacy policy. Images, figures and the PDF file itself are not sent.

API keys

API keys you enter are stored in your browser's localStorage and are sent only to the matching service, as credentials for its requests.

Local cache

Translations and summaries are cached in your browser's IndexedDB. Clearing the site's data or removing the extension deletes them.

Website access

  • The extension gets temporary access to a tab (activeTab) only when you click the paper2zh button on it, or open a link with its context menu item. It uses that access to download the PDF with your session on that site.
  • If the download still fails, the extension asks for your permission before accessing that site. You can revoke it at any time on Chrome's extensions page.
  • The extension does not read your browsing history, does not inject ads or analytics into pages, and does not access websites in the background.

Analytics and ads

paper2zh contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code, and does not sell or transfer user data.
